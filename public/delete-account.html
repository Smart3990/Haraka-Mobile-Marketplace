Delete your Haraka account

Haraka (by Haraka App) · Last updated: 18 October 2026

You can delete your account yourself at any time, in the Haraka app or on this website. You do not need to have the app installed.

Open Haraka to delete my account

Steps

  1. Open the Haraka app, or go to harakaapp.web.app on any phone or computer.
  2. Log in with the email and password of the account you want to delete.
  3. Go to Profile and tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete Forever.

Your account is deleted straight away. Forgot your password? Use Forgot password on the login screen first.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password) and your profile: name, phone, WhatsApp, photo, bio, region, saved ads, followed sellers and block list.
  • All your ads.
  • Your partner (seller or artisan) application, including your Ghana Card details and document photos.
  • Your public artisan profile, if you have one.
  • Your notification token.

What is kept

  • Messages, offers and bookings you shared with another person stay in that person's history, so they keep a record of their own dealings.
  • Reports you sent about ads or people may be kept so admins can keep the marketplace safe.
  • Copies in backups are removed over time.

See the Privacy Policy for more about how we handle your information.
