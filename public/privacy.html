Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Summary. Haraka is a marketplace for buying and selling items and booking artisans in Ghana. We collect what we need to run your account, show your ads, connect you with buyers, sellers and artisans, and keep the marketplace safe. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party advertising.

This policy explains how Haraka App ("Haraka", "we", "us") handles personal information when you use the Haraka mobile app and website (the "Service"). We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and never visible to us).
  • Profile details: phone number, WhatsApp number, profile photo, store name, bio, region and area, if you add them.
  • Partner (seller or artisan) applications: business and trade details, Ghana Card number and photos of your identity documents, and any work photos or voice introduction you submit. These are used only to verify you.
  • Ads: item photos, descriptions, prices, voice descriptions and the region or area you choose.
  • Messages, offers and calls: chat messages and price offers you send. In-app voice calls travel directly between the two phones and are not recorded; we only keep basic call details (who called whom, when, and whether it was answered).
  • Bookings and reviews: service requests, dates, locations, notes, photos, voice notes, and the ratings and reviews you write.
  • Reports and blocks: details you send when you report an ad or a person, and the list of people you blocked.

Information collected when you use the Service

  • Location: if you allow it, your approximate location is used to suggest your region. You can refuse and choose a region yourself.
  • Notification token: a device code that lets us send you notifications about messages, offers, calls and bookings.
  • Saved and followed items: the ads you save and the sellers you follow.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera and photos: to add photos to ads, your profile, applications and bookings.
  • Microphone: for voice notes and in-app voice calls, only while you are using those features.
  • Location: to suggest your region (optional).
  • Notifications: to alert you about messages, offers, calls and bookings (optional).

You can change these permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and show your ads and profile.
  • To let buyers, sellers and artisans contact each other by chat, offers and calls.
  • To review partner applications and verify identities, which helps prevent fraud.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To investigate reports, remove harmful content and keep the marketplace safe.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. What other people can see

  • Public: your ads, seller or artisan name, profile photo, store name, region, ratings and reviews, and the phone numbers you choose to show on ads or your artisan profile.
  • Only the people you deal with: your messages, offers, calls and bookings.
  • Only you and Haraka admins: your email address, partner application and identity documents.

5. Service providers we use

We use trusted providers to run the Service. They process data on our behalf and only as needed:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database and website hosting).
  • Cloudinary (storing and delivering ad photos, profile photos and voice notes).
  • Expo with Google and Apple notification services (delivering push notifications).
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (turning your approximate location into a town or region name, only if you allow location).
  • Google STUN servers (helping two phones find each other to connect an in-app call; call audio is not sent to us).

Some of these providers store data outside Ghana. We rely on their security and data protection commitments when doing so.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the providers listed above, with other users as described in section 4, or when required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, sign-in, ads, partner application and identity documents, and public artisan profile. Messages and bookings you shared with another person stay in that person's history. Copies in backups are removed over time.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: you can view and edit your profile in the app.
  • Delete: open Profile → Delete Account in the app to permanently delete your account and data.
  • Notifications and permissions: turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 you may also ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or object to how we use it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to the database is restricted by security rules, so people only see what they are allowed to see, and identity documents are visible only to you and Haraka admins. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

10. Children

Haraka is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please report it and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make important changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.

12. Contact

This Service is operated by Haraka App. A dedicated support email will be published here soon. Until then, you can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account, and you can report problems with an ad using Report this listing in the app.
